    Method

    How these numbers were made

    Solana (FARTCOIN, BOME, SPX on Solana). One getProgramAccounts call on the public Solana RPC lists every token account of the mint with its owner and balance; balances are summed per owner. Owners that are program-derived addresses (pools, vaults, lockers) are removed. For the 40 largest remaining owners of each coin, a wallet whose last 1,000 transactions happened within 24 hours is treated as an exchange or market maker and removed.

    SPX on Ethereum and KEYCAT on Base. Balances come from replaying every transfer since launch. Hand-labelled pools and exchanges (76 for SPX) and the behavioural exchange-like wallets are removed. SPX on Base (about 3% of its value) is not included, so SPX's counts are slightly low.

    Prices, market caps and volume are one CoinGecko fetch plus its free 365-day history. Volume includes centralised exchanges.

    What this can't show yet. A snapshot says who holds now, not who bought or sold. The 90-day flow analysis done for KEYCAT and SPX needs the full transfer history, which isn't available for free on Solana. Daily snapshots from today would start measuring it.
