    02 · Underwater

    How many holders are underwater

    Every coin still held is traced to the price it was received at (first in, first out). A holder is underwater when the remaining coins cost more, on average, than KEYCAT trades for today.

    03 · Buying pressure

    Who is buying and who is selling on the DEX pools

    Coins leaving a KEYCAT pool or aggregator to a wallet are buys; coins sent into one are sells. Arbitrage bots buy on one pool and sell on another, so each wallet's trades are netted over the week first. A wallet counts as a buyer or seller only if its weekly net is at least $5.

    Method

    How these numbers were made

    Data. Every KEYCAT Transfer event on Base since launch ( transfers), read from public Base RPC nodes and sorted by block and log index, so ordering is exact. Daily price is the Uniswap V2 KEYCAT/WETH pool's reserves at 12:00 UTC times Coinbase's ETH/USD close. It matches GeckoTerminal's daily closes to a median 0.3% over the last year.

    Cost basis. Each wallet keeps its coins as lots in the order received, and a send uses up the oldest lots first. A coin received from anyone, including airdrops and transfers from another wallet, carries that day's market price. That is the standard realized-price convention, so a holder who moved coins between their own wallets looks like a fresh buyer.

    Excluded. Pools (Uniswap V2/V3/V4, Aerodrome), the null address and the burn address are not holders. No exchange wallet is labelled for KEYCAT on Base explorers, so infrastructure is found by behaviour. Counting only transfers of at least 1,000 KEYCAT, a wallet that sent 300 or more and received 100 or more, to at least 100 and from at least 25 different wallets, is market infrastructure.

    Wallets, not people. One person can own many wallets. About 870,000 addresses hold less than 1 KEYCAT, mostly spam dust, so a holder here means at least 1 KEYCAT. The size tiers (10k, 100k, 1M) show where the real money is. Balances were checked against live on-chain balanceOf for the largest wallets and match to the wei.
